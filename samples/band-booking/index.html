Questions

How far ahead should we book?

Saturdays from May to October fill first, often a year ahead. Weekday and winter dates are usually easier to get.

Do you bring your own sound system?

Yes, for rooms up to about 250 guests. For bigger rooms we work with the venue's system or bring in extra sound, which is quoted separately.

Can we choose the songs?

Send us your must-play and do-not-play lists. We'll build the sets around them and learn one new song for your first dance.

How much space do you need?

About 16 by 12 feet for the full band and two standard power outlets near the stage.
