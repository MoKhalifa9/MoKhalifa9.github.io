Your quote

What affects the price

"From" prices are for a Riverbend venue on a weekday or winter date. Your written quote can change with:

  • Travel

    Venues more than 50 miles away add travel time, and overnight trips add hotel costs.

  • Sound system

    Our system covers up to 250 guests. Larger rooms or outdoor stages need extra sound and an engineer.

  • Date

    Saturdays from May to October and holiday weekends are in highest demand and priced higher.

  • Extra time

    Want one more song? Extra time is charged per half hour, agreed before the night.
