FAQ

What can the $120 fix pack include?

Fixes from the list above, such as mobile layout, tap-to-call, HTTPS, a quote form and content updates. I look at your site first and tell you which ones it needs. If the list is too long for one pack, I'll say so and quote it before you pay anything.

When does the 48 hours start?

When we've agreed the scope in writing, the first 50% is paid, and I have the access I need to your site.

Do I have to pay anything monthly?

Not to me. If your site needs hosting or a domain name, you pay those companies directly, and the accounts stay in your name.

Will you need my passwords?

Only the access the job needs. Where your website platform allows it, the safest way is to add me as a separate user, then remove me when we're done.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer in US dollars, or in USDC if you prefer. You pay 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, and I can send you an invoice for each payment.

What if you can't fix it?

If I can't deliver the fix we agreed on, I refund your deposit in full.

You're in Cairo. Does the time difference matter?

Cairo is several hours ahead of the US, so work often happens while you're asleep. We'll agree everything by email, so you have it in writing.
